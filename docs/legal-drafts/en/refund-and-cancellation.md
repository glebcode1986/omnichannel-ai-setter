## 1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy explains how to cancel a subscription to {{PRODUCT_NAME}}, what happens when a payment
fails, and when money is returned.

1.2. It forms part of the Public Offer / Service Agreement and uses the definitions in it.

1.3. **Nothing in this Policy removes a right the law gives you and does not allow to be removed.** Where
this Policy and such a right conflict, the right prevails.

## 2. What you are buying

2.1. A subscription is access to running software for a period, at the capacity your Plan allows. **It is
not a quantity of goods and not a balance of units that can be left unused.**

2.2. That matters for refunds in a specific way: once a period has begun, the Service has been made
available for it. The question a refund asks is not how much of an allowance you consumed, but whether the
Service was available to you and whether a refund is owed.

2.3. Voice-transcription top-ups are different: they are one-time purchases of an allowance, and clause 9
deals with them separately.

## 3. Cancellation

3.1. You may cancel at any time, from the Service.

3.2. **Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.** You keep access for
the rest of that period; it then does not renew.

3.3. Cancelling is not a refund request. If you want the current period refunded as well, say so — clause
7 applies.

3.4. **Cancelling deletes nothing.** Your inbox and history, knowledge, writing-style material and pipeline
remain stored and readable according to your access rights. Deletion is a separate request, described in
the Privacy Policy.

3.5. After the paid period ends, paid activity stops: preparing replies, automated sending and
transcription. Messages your customers send continue to be received and stored.

## 4. When a renewal payment fails

4.1. If a renewal payment fails, a grace period of **3 days** applies.

4.2. During the grace period the Service continues to receive and store messages, and you can correct the
payment.

4.3. After the grace period ends without a successful payment, paid activity stops. **Your data is not
deleted** and remains readable according to your access rights.

4.4. A failed payment is not a cancellation. You can resume by paying, and the Workspace continues from
its retained state.

## 5. Downgrade

5.1. **A downgrade takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for**, and **no refund is
given for the remainder of that period** merely because you asked to downgrade.

5.2. If a downgrade leaves you above your new capacity, nothing is deleted. The Service shows what exceeds
the new limits, prevents you creating more beyond them, and lets you choose which Business Profiles stay
active.

5.3. Removing a recurring add-on takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.

## 6. The trial

6.1. The trial lasts 14 days and requires no payment card, so **there is nothing to refund if you do not
continue.**

6.2. When the trial ends without a paid Plan, nothing is deleted and paid activity stops. You can still
sign in, look at what is stored, and choose a Plan.

## 8. When a refund is not given

> **Unresolved — this clause requires legal advice, and carries the greater risk of the two.** An
> exclusion that mandatory law does not permit is not merely unenforceable: it is a finding against the
> seller. Nothing here has been checked against the law that will apply.

8.1. Subject to clause 1.3 and to any right under clause 7.3(c), a refund is not ordinarily given:

   (a) for a period that has been served, where the Service was available;
   (b) because an allowance was not used — see clause 2.1;
   (c) because a downgrade or cancellation was requested mid-period — see clauses 3.2 and 5.1;
   (d) because a messaging platform changed its rules, restricted an account, or withdrew access;
   (e) because text the Service prepared was not to your liking, where the Service functioned as
       described;
   (f) where the account was suspended for a breach of the Acceptable Use Policy;
   (g) for a third party's charges, including a payment provider's fees or your own bank's charges.

8.2. On (d) specifically: a platform withdrawing access is outside our control and is addressed as force
majeure in the Public Offer. Whether it nonetheless gives rise to a remedy is a question for the governing
law, not for this clause alone.

8.3. **A provider's cost is not a reason to refuse a refund the law requires.** We incur costs with
third-party providers for work already done, and that is our commercial risk, not an exception to your
rights.

## 9. Top-ups and add-ons

9.1. A voice-transcription top-up is a one-time purchase of minutes.

9.2. Top-up minutes are used only after the minutes included with your Plan for the current period are
exhausted. **Unused top-up minutes carry over to the end of the next billing period and then expire.**

9.3. A refund of a top-up follows clauses 7 and 8. Where minutes from the top-up have been used, any refund
is reduced accordingly.

9.4. A recurring add-on is treated as part of the subscription for the purposes of this Policy.

## 10. How to request a refund

10.1. Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the e-mail address on the account, or from an address a Workspace
owner or administrator can be identified by.

10.2. Tell us which charge you mean — the date and amount are enough — and why. **Do not send card
numbers, full bank details or passwords.** We do not need them and we do not want them.

10.3. We may ask for information needed to identify the payment or to establish the circumstances.

## 11. How a request is decided

11.1. We will decide within {{REFUND_DECISION_DAYS}} of receiving the information needed.

11.2. We will give a reason for a refusal.

11.3. We may consult our records of the Service's availability and of the activity on the account. Those
records hold identifiers, outcomes and counts rather than the content of your conversations.

## 12. How a refund is paid

12.1. An approved refund is returned by the same method as the original payment, through the payment
provider that took it.

12.2. We will instruct the refund within {{REFUND_PAYOUT_DAYS}} of approving it. How long it then takes to
appear is the payment provider's and your bank's, not ours.

12.3. A refund takes effect in the Service once we have verified it on our own side. A redirect or a
provider's notification alone is not treated as proof.

## 13. Exceptional refunds

13.1. We may give a refund where this Policy does not require one, if the circumstances warrant it.

13.2. Doing so once does not create an entitlement and is not a precedent.

## 14. Chargebacks and disputes

14.1. If you believe a charge is wrong, please write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} first. Most problems are quicker
to fix directly than through a bank.

14.2. Where a dispute is raised with a payment provider, **we may restrict paid automated activity on the
Workspace while the dispute is examined.** Messages your customers send continue to be received and
stored.

14.3. We will respond to a dispute with our records of the purchase, the Plan and period it paid for, the
record of acceptance of our legal documents, and the availability of the Service.

14.4. **A chargeback does not delete your data.**

## 15. What a refund does not do

15.1. **It does not delete anything.** A refund, a chargeback, a downgrade, a cancellation and a failed
payment all leave your data in place. Deletion is a separate request, described in the Privacy Policy.

15.2. It does not remove the record of your acceptance of our legal documents. Those records cannot be
altered, because evidence of consent that can be edited is not evidence.

15.3. It does not revoke a later subscription — see clause 7.5.
