## 2. Definitions

**Service** — {{PRODUCT_NAME}}, available at {{SERVICE_URL}}.

**Workspace** — the Customer's account in the Service, containing its Business Profiles, members and
content.

**Business Profile** — the unit the Service is sold by. One Business Profile may hold up to one Instagram
account and up to one WhatsApp Business account or number at the same time.

**Member** — a person the Customer has invited to its Workspace, holding a role.

**Draft mode** — automation in which the Service prepares a reply and does not send it until a Member
reviews, edits or approves it.

**Autopilot mode** — automation in which the Service may send a reply without prior review by a Member.

**Review Queue** — where the Service collects what needs a person.

**Takeover** — a Member assuming control of a conversation, which stops automated sending in it.

**Plan** — a named, versioned set of capacity limits, included allowances and features.

**Billing interval** — monthly or annual.

**Add-on** — a recurring purchase that increases capacity.

**Top-up** — a one-time purchase that increases an allowance.

**Billing period** — the period paid for by one charge.

**Published price list** — the prices shown in the Service for each Plan, interval and currency.

## 8. The trial

8.1. A trial of **14 days** is available, and **no payment card is required** to start it.

8.2. **The trial begins when the Workspace successfully connects its first Business Profile**, not when an
account is created.

8.3. During the trial the Customer has one Business Profile, up to three Member seats, the Service's core
functionality including Draft and Autopilot modes, and **100 minutes** of voice transcription.

8.4. The Service shows the days and allowance remaining.

8.5. **When a trial ends without a paid Plan, nothing is deleted.** The inbox and history, knowledge,
writing-style material and pipeline remain readable according to the Customer's access rights. What stops
is paid activity: preparing replies, automated sending and transcription. On payment, the Workspace
resumes from its retained state.

## 9. Capacity, allowances and top-ups

9.1. A Plan sets hard capacity limits — Business Profile slots and Member seats — and included allowances.

9.2. **Voice transcription is the only allowance the Customer is metered on.** Each Plan includes a number
of minutes per billing period.

9.3. **When the included transcription allowance is exhausted:** text conversations and the ordinary
behaviour of the Service continue; an incoming voice message is still stored and visible; it is not
transcribed automatically unless further allowance is available; the Service explains that the allowance
is exhausted; and the Customer may handle the message itself or buy a top-up.

9.4. **No automatic overage charge is applied.** Exhausting an allowance never produces a charge the
Customer did not choose.

9.5. Add-ons increase capacity. They do **not** change the feature class of the Plan: a Plan with an extra
Business Profile still has that Plan's analytics, retention and controls.

9.6. A recurring Add-on bought part-way through a billing period is apportioned for the remainder of that
period where the payment provider supports apportionment reliably. Removing a recurring Add-on takes
effect at the end of the period already paid for.

9.7. Top-up minutes are consumed only after the Plan's included minutes for the current billing period are
exhausted. **Unused top-up minutes carry over to the end of the next billing period and then expire.**

9.8. Where a Plan with Add-ons costs as much as a larger Plan offering more, the Service may recommend the
larger Plan. **It will not change the Customer's Plan without being asked.**

## 10. Renewal, failed payment and grace

10.1. A subscription renews automatically for the same interval at the price then applying to the Plan and
interval purchased, unless cancelled.

10.2. Where a renewal payment fails, a grace period of **3 days** applies. During it, messages continue to
be received and stored.

10.3. **After the grace period ends without a successful payment, paid activity stops** — preparing
replies, automated sending and transcription. **Retained data is not deleted** and remains readable
according to the Customer's access rights.

10.4. A change of entitlement follows from a payment the Provider has verified on its own side. **A
redirect back from a payment page is not proof of payment**, and the Service does not treat it as one.

## 11. Upgrade, downgrade and cancellation

11.1. An upgrade takes effect once payment is confirmed.

11.2. **A downgrade takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.** No refund is given for the
remainder of that period merely because a downgrade was requested.

11.3. **Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for** unless the Customer asks
otherwise and the Provider agrees. The Service remains available for the rest of the period.

11.4. Where a downgrade leaves the Workspace above its new capacity, nothing is deleted. The Service shows
what exceeds the new limits, prevents new resources being created beyond them, and lets the Customer
choose which Business Profiles stay active. Underlying channel connections remain stored.

11.5. **A downgrade, a cancellation, a failed payment, a refund or a chargeback does not delete the
Customer's data.**

## 13. The Customer's acknowledgments about automated assistance

The Customer acknowledges that:

13.1. The Service prepares replies using third-party models, and those replies can be wrong, incomplete or
unsuitable.

13.2. **In Autopilot mode, messages are sent to the Customer's customers without a person reading them
first**, and the Customer is responsible for them as if it had sent them itself.

13.3. In Draft mode nothing is sent until a Member approves it.

13.4. The Service applies determinate safety rules and holds a reply for a person where they are not
satisfied, including in the high-risk categories named in the Terms of Service. These rules reduce risk;
they do not eliminate it.

13.5. A transcript of a voice note is a machine's reading of a recording and may be wrong. Where a reply
would answer a recording whose words are unavailable, the Service does not send it without a person.

13.6. Messaging platforms restrict when and how a business may reply, and the Service will decline to send
rather than breach those restrictions. Delivery is not immediate and is not guaranteed.

13.7. A provider or platform may be unavailable, and that is an expected operating condition rather than a
breach of this Agreement.
