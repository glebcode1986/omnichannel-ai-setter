5.2. **Autopilot sends messages to your customers without a person reading them first.** That is what it
is for, and you are responsible for the messages it sends in your name, as if you had sent them yourself.

5.4. The assistant answers from the knowledge you give it and from the conversation in front of it. It is

5.5. **A person always outranks the automation.** When a member takes a conversation over — by writing in

correlate it to something the Service sent, the Service treats that as a person having taken over.

6.3. The Service may group several messages that arrive together and answer them once, and may delay a

6.4. Delivery depends on the platform. A message accepted by the Service may be delayed, refused or lost
by the platform, and a delivery or read receipt is the platform's statement rather than ours.

6.5. **Scheduled follow-ups.** Within a conversation somebody has already started, the Service can schedule

and platform restrictions apply. It is not a way to reach anybody who has not written to you, and the
Service has no mechanism for that.
